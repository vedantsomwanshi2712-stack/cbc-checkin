# CBC Check-in

The check-in page for **Claude Builder Club, IIT Delhi** meetings.

**Check in here:** https://vedantsomwanshi2712-stack.github.io/cbc-checkin/

## How to check in

1. Open the link above or scan the QR code shown at the meeting.
2. Fill in the form and submit.
3. You're checked in.

## Good to know

- Check-in is only open **during club meetings**. At other times the page shows "Check-in is closed."
- Please check in only if you're at the meeting.
- Missed it or ran into a problem? Talk to someone from the CBC team at the meeting.
